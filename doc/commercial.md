# Commercial Use & Licensing

Minepanel is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](https://github.com/Ketbome/minepanel/blob/main/LICENSE).
Companies can use it under those terms, or contact me for a commercial agreement.

## Using it under the AGPL-3.0

Free, including for commercial use. Run it for your community, your company or your
customers. The main obligation: if you modify Minepanel and let people use it over a network,
you must offer them the source code of your modified version under the same license.

The name and logo are covered separately by the
[trademark policy](https://github.com/Ketbome/minepanel/blob/main/TRADEMARK.md).
